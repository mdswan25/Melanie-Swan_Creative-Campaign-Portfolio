Case Study 02 IHG Business Rewards · IHG Hotels & Resorts

A loyalty campaign that turned business travel from a cost of the job into the down payment on a dream trip.

My role
Associate Global Brand Manager, Loyalty
Years
2015 — 2018
Channels
Email · App · LinkedIn · Airport OOH · Hotel training
The Big Idea

Business travel feels like a sacrifice nobody sees. The campaign makes that sacrifice visible and rewarding by pairing every Monday of the work week with the Saturday it pays for. Benefits, bonus offers and gamified challenges all point to one promise: the work you're already doing is earning your escape.

The Creative Brief

Field guide · one page
Insight

Business travel feels like a cost. Members stay engaged when they can see it turning into something personal.

Proposition

Every business stay brings your next escape closer.

Objective

Grow engagement, satisfaction and enrollment for IHG Business Rewards across 100+ countries and a 13-brand hotel portfolio.

Audience

Small-business owners and frequent business travelers who book often but don't think of themselves as “loyalty people.”

Reasons to believe

Points on qualifying stays, bonus-point offers, 13 hotel brands to redeem across, and hotel teams trained to enroll and recognize members.

Mandatories

Global brand standards, program terms on every offer, and creative that works for both the business owner and the traveler.

Deliverables

Key visual, lifecycle emails, app challenges, LinkedIn sponsored posts, airport lightboxes, hotel training webinars and videos.

Measure & learn

Engagement, member satisfaction, monthly enrollment growth and hotel training participation.

The Manifesto
The 5 a.m. alarms. The middle seat.
The hotel desk at midnight with one more deck to finish.

Nobody sees that part of the job. We do.

So every night on the road counts toward something you'll want to remember: a terrace above the sea, a bath with a view, a pool that runs into the horizon.

You've been earning it all year. Go collect.
Long-form copy · member welcome film · enrollment landing page
