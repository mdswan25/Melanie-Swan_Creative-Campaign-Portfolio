How I Build a Campaign

Brief to results

Every campaign here starts with the same one-page brief: an objective, an audience, one insight, one proposition, and the proof to back it up. Then the work goes out to agencies, studios and AI-assisted teams with nothing left to guess.

01
Find the human truth

Research, brand trackers and customer insight first, so the idea starts with what people want.

02
Write one big idea

A single-minded proposition and a line flexible enough for every market and channel.

03
Craft every touchpoint

Agency briefs, shoots, storyboards and toolkits, from the print ad to the push notification.

04
Prove it, then scale it

A/B and holdout testing, campaign scorecards, and honest numbers that earn the next budget.
