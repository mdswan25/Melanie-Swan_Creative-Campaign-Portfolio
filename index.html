How I Build a Campaign

Brief to results

Every campaign here starts with the same one-page brief: an objective, an audience, one insight, one proposition, and the proof to back it up. Then the work goes out to agencies, studios and AI-assisted teams with nothing left to guess.

01
Find the human truth

Research, brand trackers and customer insight first, so the idea starts with what people want.

02
Write one big idea

A single-minded proposition and a line flexible enough for every market and channel.

03
Craft every touchpoint

Agency briefs, shoots, storyboards and toolkits, from the print ad to the push notification.

04
Prove it, then scale it

A/B and holdout testing, campaign scorecards, and honest numbers that earn the next budget.

Case Study 01 Holiday Inn Family of Brands · IHG Hotels & Resorts

A beach-escape campaign built to turn summer daydreams into direct bookings, told the same way in 100+ markets.

My role
Global Brand Marketing Associate
Years
2012 — 2015
Channels
Print · OOH · Social · Email · Travel sites · Events
The Big Idea

People don't book a room. They book the moment the work brain finally switches off. So we sold that moment. One flexible line, “Nothing on the agenda but…”, lets every market finish the sentence with its own beach, pool or sunset while the promise stays the same everywhere.

The Creative Brief

Field guide · one page
Insight

Vacation doesn't start at check-in. It starts the moment you stop checking your phone.

Proposition

A Holiday Inn beach stay is the easiest way to get to “nothing on the agenda.”

Objective

Grow new-guest acquisition and direct bookings for the Holiday Inn family of brands, and lift brand awareness across 100+ markets.

Audience

Families and couples planning their one big trip of the year. They compare a lot of options and choose the one that feels like a real break.

Reasons to believe

Beachfront and resort locations, pools made for long afternoons, family-friendly stays and member perks for booking direct.

Mandatories

Global brand toolkit standards in every market. Local teams can change the photography and the offer, but never the platform line.

Deliverables

Key visual, print, OOH, social carousels, email, paid travel-site placements, grand-opening and trade-show materials.

Measure & learn

Acquisition, direct bookings, brand awareness from quarterly tracker studies, and leads from trade shows.

The Manifesto
Somewhere between the airport and the shoreline,
the to-do list goes quiet.

The emails can wait. The alarm stays off.
The only decisions left are pool or beach,
sunrise or sunset, one more swim or two.

This is the trip you'll still be talking about in December.
Nothing on the agenda but the ocean.
Long-form copy · :60 brand film voiceover · magazine spread

Case Study 02 IHG Business Rewards · IHG Hotels & Resorts

A loyalty campaign that turned business travel from a cost of the job into the down payment on a dream trip.

My role
Associate Global Brand Manager, Loyalty
Years
2015 — 2018
Channels
Email · App · LinkedIn · Airport OOH · Hotel training
The Big Idea

Business travel feels like a sacrifice nobody sees. The campaign makes that sacrifice visible and rewarding by pairing every Monday of the work week with the Saturday it pays for. Benefits, bonus offers and gamified challenges all point to one promise: the work you're already doing is earning your escape.

The Creative Brief

Field guide · one page
Insight

Business travel feels like a cost. Members stay engaged when they can see it turning into something personal.

Proposition

Every business stay brings your next escape closer.

Objective

Grow engagement, satisfaction and enrollment for IHG Business Rewards across 100+ countries and a 13-brand hotel portfolio.

Audience

Small-business owners and frequent business travelers who book often but don't think of themselves as “loyalty people.”

Reasons to believe

Points on qualifying stays, bonus-point offers, 13 hotel brands to redeem across, and hotel teams trained to enroll and recognize members.

Mandatories

Global brand standards, program terms on every offer, and creative that works for both the business owner and the traveler.

Deliverables

Key visual, lifecycle emails, app challenges, LinkedIn sponsored posts, airport lightboxes, hotel training webinars and videos.

Measure & learn

Engagement, member satisfaction, monthly enrollment growth and hotel training participation.

The Manifesto
The 5 a.m. alarms. The middle seat.
The hotel desk at midnight with one more deck to finish.

Nobody sees that part of the job. We do.

So every night on the road counts toward something you'll want to remember: a terrace above the sea, a bath with a view, a pool that runs into the horizon.

You've been earning it all year. Go collect.
Long-form copy · member welcome film · enrollment landing page

Case Study 03 Kao Corporation · Salon Division

A pro-haircare campaign that sells the feeling first, so clients rebook the service and take the product home.

My role
Senior Brand Manager, Loyalty (Contract)
Years
2022 — 2023
Channels
Print · In-salon · Social · Retail · Pro loyalty · Trade shows
The Big Idea

Stylists don't sell products. They share what made a client feel looked after. So the campaign treats the service like a spa ritual and the products as the way to bring that feeling home. The same idea runs the pro loyalty program, which rewards the stylists who make the ritual unforgettable.

The Creative Brief

Field guide · one page
Insight

The wash is the part clients remember most, and the part salons sell least.

Proposition

The ritual is the reason they rebook, and the reason they buy.

Objective

Make the salon loyalty program a growth engine: more active professionals, more revenue and more share in premium haircare.

Audience

Salon professionals who choose the brands they use and recommend, and the clients who buy what their stylist trusts.

Reasons to believe

Professional formulas, education and launch support for salons, rewards and tiers for pros, and real client reviews.

Mandatories

Salon-exclusive positioning, brand guidelines and tone of voice, and claims that are approved before they run.

Deliverables

Key visual, print, in-salon posters and mirror clings, social, take-home retail displays, pro program assets, trade-show booth.

Measure & learn

Active engagement rate, revenue, market share and ROI on paid and owned channels.

The Manifesto
Warm water. A slow scalp massage.
The scent of something good.

For ten minutes, nobody needs anything from you.

That's the part clients remember,
and the reason they come back.

We make the ritual. Stylists make it unforgettable.
Long-form copy · in-salon video loop · launch film

Case Study 04 Inspire Brands · Arby's · Buffalo Wild Wings · Dunkin' · Jimmy John's · SONIC

A playful rewards campaign built on one shared engine, so five restaurant brands could grow customer value without losing their personality.

My role
Enterprise Loyalty & Customer Marketing Manager
Years
2024 — 2025
Channels
App · Social · OOH · Partnerships · Personalization
The Big Idea

Cravings aren't loyal to one menu, so rewards shouldn't be either. I recommended “one engine, five front doors”: a shared segmentation and lifecycle backbone behind the scenes, while each brand keeps its own creative, voice and rewards. Leadership adopted and funded it, and the campaign gave it a voice customers would actually enjoy.

The Strategy

One engine.
Five front doors.

The Creative Brief

Field guide · one page
Insight

People don't want another loyalty program. They want their cravings to be noticed.

Proposition

Rewards that know your order, behind five brands you already love.

Objective

Grow customer lifetime value and loyalty penetration across a five-brand restaurant portfolio, without making the brands look or sound alike.

Audience

Busy app and drive-thru regulars who love more than one of the brands, from an Arby's lunch to a Dunkin' morning run, a Jimmy John's delivery, a SONIC happy hour or Buffalo Wild Wings on game night, but treat each one as a separate relationship.

Reasons to believe

Personalized offers, earn-and-burn bonus points, gamified challenges and partner perks with JetBlue and Fandango.

Mandatories

Each brand's own voice and rewards stay intact. Shared data and offers run quietly in the background.

Deliverables

Key visual, app challenges, social, OOH, partner co-marketing, cross-brand promotions and a loyalty calendar.

Measure & learn

Customer lifetime value, loyalty penetration, revenue across program, digital and partner streams, and digital CSAT.

The Manifesto
You know your order. We know your order.

The Tuesday burger. The 7 a.m. cold brew.
The milkshake you said you'd skip.

Every craving is a chance to say thanks,
so we built rewards that remember what you like
and show up right when you want it.

Your cravings called. We picked up.
Long-form copy · app onboarding · :15 social spots

The Campaign

OOH · App · Social · Partnerships
Why it works
Fun on the outside. Smart underneath.

The voice is playful, but every message is triggered by the shared engine: segment, daypart and past orders decide who sees the shake, who sees the coffee and when the challenge appears. AI-driven personalization made the offers feel personal, and I made the budget case for it directly to the CMO.
