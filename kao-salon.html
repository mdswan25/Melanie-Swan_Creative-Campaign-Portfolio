Case Study 03 Kao Corporation · Salon Division

A pro-haircare campaign that sells the feeling first, so clients rebook the service and take the product home.

My role
Senior Brand Manager, Loyalty (Contract)
Years
2022 — 2023
Channels
Print · In-salon · Social · Retail · Pro loyalty · Trade shows
The Big Idea

Stylists don't sell products. They share what made a client feel looked after. So the campaign treats the service like a spa ritual and the products as the way to bring that feeling home. The same idea runs the pro loyalty program, which rewards the stylists who make the ritual unforgettable.

The Creative Brief

Field guide · one page
Insight

The wash is the part clients remember most, and the part salons sell least.

Proposition

The ritual is the reason they rebook, and the reason they buy.

Objective

Make the salon loyalty program a growth engine: more active professionals, more revenue and more share in premium haircare.

Audience

Salon professionals who choose the brands they use and recommend, and the clients who buy what their stylist trusts.

Reasons to believe

Professional formulas, education and launch support for salons, rewards and tiers for pros, and real client reviews.

Mandatories

Salon-exclusive positioning, brand guidelines and tone of voice, and claims that are approved before they run.

Deliverables

Key visual, print, in-salon posters and mirror clings, social, take-home retail displays, pro program assets, trade-show booth.

Measure & learn

Active engagement rate, revenue, market share and ROI on paid and owned channels.
